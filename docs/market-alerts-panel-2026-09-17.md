# Piyasa Uyarıları: concise, source-linked explanations

The dashboard previously combined a heuristic news list with a separate unvalidated AI-wide risk score, sentiment and stock entry labels. The two requests could reflect different news snapshots. Questions in headlines could be presented alongside apparently confirmed market developments.

The existing Piyasa Uyarıları section now uses one request, started only when expanded. It shows three stories initially, source links and publication times in Europe/Istanbul, topic filters and a show-all control. Each story has native keyboard-accessible expandable details: conditional positive and negative channels, relevant sectors when applicable, what to check next and timing considerations. Templates are topic-based educational scenarios, not news-specific LLM analysis or calibrated forecasts. Unsupported topics explicitly have no scenario. The legacy news-analysis service remains for its separate evening-analysis consumer; the dashboard no longer requests it. The retired development radar and its background notifications remain removed.

The server filters invalid/future/unverified dates, items over 48 hours old and low-importance items. It orders before limiting, deduplicates tracking URLs and titles, creates stable IDs and restricts links to the existing feed providers. A priority label means topic importance from existing keywords, not measured financial risk. Source failure, empty eligible feed, slow requests and stale retained client results have separate messages. A stalled request aborts after 20 seconds; collapsing cancels the request and late responses are ignored.

Scenario rationale: financing, demand and exchange-rate channels are described in [TCMB's monetary policy overview](https://www.tcmb.gov.tr/wps/wcm/connect/tr/tcmb%2Btr/main%2Bmenu/temel%2Bfaaliyetler/para%2Bpolitikasi) and [TCMB's post-crisis monetary policy working paper](https://www.tcmb.gov.tr/wps/wcm/connect/f352ae97-2652-4505-b4ec-c177547b9360/WP1217.pdf?MOD=AJPERES). The distinction between crude oil and jet-fuel cost and airline exposure is explained on [IATA's economics page](https://www.iata.org/en/publications/economics/). The application's conditional wording is an implementation inference from these mechanisms, not a provider's prediction about any displayed security.

Validation: unit coverage for questions, conditional scenarios, freshness, unsafe URLs, stable IDs, deduplication, ranking and source failures; component coverage for deferred loading, three-story display, filters, source links, retry, cancellation and deadline. Existing dashboard tests continue to pass. No new database fields or migrations. No live-device visual validation was performed in this environment.
